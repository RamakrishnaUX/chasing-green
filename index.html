Chasing Green

Drive. Wait. Look up.

A quiet road trip around Iceland. Find a dark spot under a clear sky, check the radio, and watch for the lights.

WASD or arrows to drive. Space for nitro. C to change camera or step out. X to sit. V to switch car. M for the map. H to learn about the aurora.
